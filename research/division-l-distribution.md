# DIVISION L: Distribution Mechanics (getting the deck seen)

Synthesized from four scout sweeps (L1 caption/SEO, L2 DM funnels, L3 posting mechanics, L4 LinkedIn crosspost) plus division-head verification searches on 2026-07-10. Recency window: April to July 2026 weighted heaviest; older material labeled background.

Scope reminder: Division L does not touch what a slide LOOKS like. It answers a narrower question, once the deck exists, what maximizes how many humans see it and act on it. Design divisions decide the poster; L decides the reach.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. Hashtags are dead as a reach lever and now hard-capped at 5 (platform-enforced, full rollout by end of Jan 2026). Reach moved into saves, DM-sends, and keyword text.
2. Instagram OCRs on-image text and feeds it into Search, so one giant keyword headline per poster slide is INDEXED, the poster redesign costs zero keyword surface.
3. The comment-keyword DM funnel is the single best-aligned 2026 mechanic (fabricates early comments, dodges the link-in-bio penalty, 40 to 70 percent DM click-through), keep it on every deck.
4. ManyChat's March 2 2026 contact-based repricing taxes audience growth (free tier gutted to 25 contacts), flat-rate official-API tools (InstantDM ~8 to 10 USD, LinkDM 19 USD) do the identical job.
5. Post education decks Tuesday or Wednesday late morning to midday; hold cadence at 3 to 5x per week, not daily (7x+ correlates with higher unfollows).
6. Design for SENDS then SAVES, the two signals that both travel to non-followers and mark a deck as boostable. Carter's own image-artifact decks (2,700 to 6,600 saves) already prove this.
7. There is NO native carousel trial, so the cover must be right first try; run the hook as a Trial Reel first if you want a non-follower pre-signal.
8. Same 1080x1350 render crossposts to LinkedIn as a PDF document post, the highest-engagement, lowest-supply LinkedIn format (6.6 to 7.0 percent, only ~4.88 percent of creators post them). Near-free upside.
9. Google has indexed public professional-account IG posts since 2025-07-10, the caption's first 125 chars is now the Google snippet, write it as a searched query.
10. Instrumentation is the real gap, tag every commenter by deck+keyword+outcome so decks rank by funnel yield, not vanity reach.

## THE FINDINGS THAT MATTER (ranked)

### 1. On-image text is OCR-indexed, so poster slides do NOT cost SEO (the finding that unblocks the whole redesign)
Instagram's algorithm reads on-screen/on-image text via OCR and factors it into Search and recommendations. A poster slide with one massive headline is indexed the same as body copy. The scary premise behind busy 6-to-9-zone slides, that more text zones equal more indexable keywords, is false. Going poster-style is fully compatible with search, provided the few big words are the SEARCHED words (a job title, a cert name spelled in full, a tool name, a salary number), not a vibe phrase.
Sources: SEO Sherpa (seosherpa.com/instagram-search-results), TrueFuture Media, Aurelius Media, multiple 2026 algorithm guides (L1).

### 2. Hashtags: hard-capped at 5, enforced, dead as reach (verified 3x)
Instagram announced a hard limit of five hashtags per post/reel; reported 2025-12-18 (Social Media Today, socialmediatoday.com/news/.../808309). Verification search added a corroborating detail: gradual global rollout, full implementation across all accounts by end of January 2026 (thedigitalperch.com, elsop.com, techbuzz.ai). The caption-plus-comments split trick is dead (limit is combined). Following hashtags was already removed Dec 2024. Mosseri: hashtags "help with categorisation, but not distribution" and, on whether they increase reach, "They don't work! I'll tell you that" (via financial-marketer.com; Buffer: "It's official: hashtags don't help reach"). Verdict: alive as a weak categorization/search signal, dead as a reach lever. Use 3 to 5 niche tags and stop optimizing them.
Sources: Social Media Today, financial-marketer.com, Buffer, thedigitalperch.com, elsop.com (L1 + head verification).

### 3. The comment-keyword DM funnel is the correct 2026 distribution mechanic
"Comment KEYWORD" simultaneously (a) manufactures early comments, the #2 engagement signal, that trigger Explore/Suggested promotion in the first 60 minutes, (b) keeps the off-platform link OUT of the caption so you dodge the ~60 percent link-in-bio/off-platform reach penalty, and (c) delivers the link at peak intent in the DM. Conversion by hop: comment-triggered DM open 85 to 92 percent (vs 40 to 55 for follow triggers); link-in-DM click 40 to 70 percent (vs 1 to 3 percent link-in-bio); in-DM email capture 60 to 85 percent. DM shares (sends per reach) are the single most weighted Reels ranking signal. This validates Carter's existing approach, it is not just fine, it is structurally optimal.
Sources: creatorflow.so (algorithm-2026, reels-to-DM, compliance), communipass auto-DM stats 2026, inro.social/blog/link-in-dm (updated 2026-05-21), Buffer, TrueFuture (L2).

### 4. ManyChat repricing makes flat-rate tools the smarter default
ManyChat moved to contact-based billing on 2026-03-02: free tier cut from 1,000 to 25 contacts (~97.5 percent), Essential 14 USD/250, Pro 29 USD/2,500, Business 69 USD/7,500; AI is now a 29 USD/mo add-on. Contact billing taxes exactly what we want (audience growth) and an IG-only creator pays for idle WhatsApp/SMS capacity. Flat-rate official-API alternatives do the identical comment-to-DM job: InstantDM ~8 to 9.99 USD unlimited contacts (Meta Business Partner), LinkDM 19 USD flat (1,000 free DMs/mo tier), Inro free tier. MobileMonkey is dead for creators (now Customers.ai, ~199 USD/mo). A viral deck pulling thousands of unique commenters is the worst case for ManyChat's model and the best case for flat-rate.
Sources: flowgent.ai/blog/manychat-pricing, creatorflow.so/blog/manychat-pricing-trap, replyrush.com, instantdm.com/blog/top-15-manychat-alternatives (L2 + head verification).

### 5. Meta's hard DM limits constrain funnel design (not the deck, but the ops)
200 automated DMs/hour per account (rolling 60-min window, down ~96 percent from 5,000) and, NEW in 2026, 1 automated DM per user per 24h from comment/Story triggers. Viral spikes QUEUE rather than drop (500 comments = 200 instant, 200 hour 2, 100 hour 3), so a banger deck does not lose leads but late commenters wait 1 to 3 hours (warm-lead decay). Two non-Meta links in one DM is a hard compliance trigger. Business/Creator account + Facebook Page is mandatory (Carter has this). Mitigation: do not stack two keyword funnels in the same hour; do not design a flow that DMs the same follower from two decks in one day; split any multi-link handoff across messages.
Sources: creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-dm-compliance-meta-rules, sumgenius ban-wave, developers.facebook.com policy (L2).

### 6. Post Tue/Wed midday, cadence 3 to 5x/week not daily
Two independent large datasets converge. Sprout Social (~2B engagements, ~307k profiles, Nov 2025 to Feb 2026): "Tuesdays and Wednesdays at 11 a.m. perform best for schools and educational institutions." Buffer (9.6M posts, updated 2026-05-14): best days Wed > Thu > Tue, carousel slots Wed 12pm / Wed 6pm / Thu 9am, worst days Fri/Sat. Cadence: 52 percent of top performers post 4x/week; 7x+/week correlates with ~25 percent higher unfollows; the real driver is consistency (accounts posting 20+ of 26 weeks saw a 450 percent engagement lift, Buffer 52M-post study).
Sources: sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram, buffer.com/resources (when-to-post + how-often) (L3).

### 7. Design for SENDS then SAVES; boost only proven organic winners
Sends >> likes for reaching non-followers (verified direction, Mosseri via Social Media Today 2025 + Later + Buffer; the exact "3 to 5x likes" multiplier is repeated everywhere but the primary Mosseri source was not found, treat the number as folklore, the direction as solid). Saves mark a deck as revisit-worthy and boostable. Never boost cold, only amplify winners, pick them by SAVES and SENDS not likes (Metricool). Carter's image-artifact decks (47k to 65k views, 2,700 to 6,600 saves) are the exact boostable profile; start at 5 to 20 USD via the Boost button, not Ads Manager.
Sources: Social Media Today (algorithm insights 2025), Later, Buffer, metricool.com/boost-instagram-posts (L3).

### 8. No native carousel trial exists; every slide must be a valid re-entry point
Trial Reels (non-followers first, 1,000+ follower public accounts, scheduling added Feb 2026, 72h auto-share) are REELS-ONLY, the official page never mentions carousels. So there is no low-risk pre-test for a carousel, the cover must land first try. Native proxy: run the deck's hook as a Trial Reel first, read the 72h non-follower signal, then build the winner as a carousel. Separately, Instagram RE-SERVES carousels to people who scrolled past, starting on a slide they did not see, so every slide must read as a standalone poster. This is an algorithmic argument for poster-not-newspaper, not just an aesthetic one, and it gives carousels a days-long tail vs reels' front-loaded burst.
Sources: creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels, socialmediatoday.com (trial-reels scheduling), truefuturemedia, contentdrips (L3).

### 9. The same render crossposts to LinkedIn as its highest-value format
1080x1350 (4:5) is Instagram-native AND a fully supported, uncropped LinkedIn document ratio, no square re-render. Document/PDF posts are the highest-engagement LinkedIn format and the lowest supply: 6.60 percent (AuthoredUp 3M-post study) and, per a second independent dataset found in verification, 7.00 percent native document engagement (Socialinsider, 1.3M posts), vs video 5.6, image 3.2, text 2.0. Only ~4.88 percent of creators post documents regularly. Saves are worth ~5x a like on LinkedIn and documents are 12.92 percent of all saved posts (~2.6x their content share). Carter's save-heavy decks are already tuned for the exact signal LinkedIn ranks hardest. Tooling: shotpls PNG -> JPEG q92 -> `img2pdf --imgsize 144dpi -o deck.pdf slide-*.jpg` (144 dpi is verified arithmetic mapping 2160x2700 px to a 1080x1350 pt page). Do NOT feed PNGs straight to img2pdf, flate-wrapping bloats the file; the JPEG intermediate is mandatory. Target 2 to 4 MB per 8 to 10 slide deck.
Sources: contentdrips.com (LinkedIn document posts, Jul 2026), dataslayer.ai (LinkedIn algorithm Feb 2026), authoredup.com, socialinsider (via head verification), josch/img2pdf README + Debian manpage (L4 + head verification).

### 10. Google indexes IG posts; caption first 125 chars is the SEO surface
Since 2025-07-10 Google indexes public posts from professional/creator accounts (18+, posts Jan 2020 on; Stories/private excluded), default ON. Google reads captions, alt text, on-screen text, hashtags, bio. The first ~125 caption chars = the Google snippet/meta description. Keyword surfaces in priority order: caption first 125 chars > on-image headline text (OCR) > alt text > bio/name field > 3 to 5 hashtags. The NAME field (bold line, not @handle) is searchable and SEO-weighted. Alt text is read by IG Search + Google/Bing Images and is likely currently unused = free upside.
Sources: Forbes (Tobin, 2025-07-10), evolare.digital, inro.social, SEO Sherpa, Later (L1).

### Contradictions and soft numbers, flagged explicitly
- "Sends weighted 3 to 5x likes": cited across influencermarketinghub, truefuturemedia, contentdrips, dataslayer, but no primary Mosseri source was located by any scout or by head verification. Direction (sends >> likes for non-follower reach) is solid; the specific multiplier is folklore. Do not quote the number as fact to anyone.
- 5-hashtag ENFORCEMENT mechanism is described inconsistently (hard block at publish vs silent removal vs distribution suppression). Instagram's verbatim announcement only says the number will be "updated to five." Reliable takeaway: over 5 = suppressed. Do not claim a specific block behavior.
- LinkedIn document engagement rate: 6.60 percent (AuthoredUp) vs 7.00 percent (Socialinsider native documents). Not a contradiction, two datasets, same direction (documents #1). Cite the range, not a single decimal.
- "First hour or it dies": folklore. Officially, Instagram acknowledges "speed of engagement," there is no literal 60-minute timer. Optimize by posting into an active window, do not build a ritual around a countdown.
- Conversion benchmarks (open/click/email rates) are near-entirely vendor/agency-sourced (creatorflow, communipass, inro, instantdm) with an incentive to inflate. Directionally consistent across independent vendors, but no neutral first-party Meta dataset. Treat as targets to test against, not guarantees.

## DIRECTIVES FOR THE REBUILD (numbered, concrete)

1. Ship a caption template in make-carousel: line 1 = keyword hook under 80 chars (the exact query a beginner Googles, e.g. "how much does a SOC analyst make"), lines 2 to 4 = value preview with secondary keywords, one specific CTA, keyword inside first 125 chars, exactly 3 to 5 niche hashtags never more.

2. Enforce a keyword-headline rule on every hero/cover slide: the biggest words must be searched terms (job title, cert name in full, tool, salary), never a vibe phrase. On-image text is OCR-indexed, so this is free SEO, do not waste it.

3. Add a per-post alt-text step to the publish checklist: one keyword-descriptive sentence per post. It is read by IG Search + Google/Bing Images and is currently unused free upside.

4. Keep the comment-keyword DM funnel on every deck, with a campaign-specific keyword (never "YES"/"OK"). Adopt the 3-message handoff: msg1 mirrors the keyword + one routing question, msg2 delivers one link with one sentence of context + asks email in-DM, msg3 routes to trial.

5. Audit the DM tool and monthly cost. If on ManyChat contact-based, price out InstantDM (~8 to 10 USD unlimited) or LinkDM (19 USD flat). A viral deck is the worst case for ManyChat's model.

6. Bake Meta's limits into ops: never stack two keyword funnels in the same hour; never design a flow that DMs the same follower from two decks in one day; split any multi-link handoff across separate DMs.

7. Lock the default posting slot to Tuesday or Wednesday late morning to midday (Sprout: Tue/Wed 11am education; Buffer: Wed 12pm carousels); secondary Thu 9am to noon; never weekends for value decks. Confirm against own Insights after 4 to 6 posts.

8. Hold cadence at 3 to 5 posts/week per handle, not daily. If the weekly 7-reel + 7-carousel slate all hits the main handle, lean the carousel side toward the 3 to 5 highest-effort poster decks over volume.

9. Design each slide to be a standalone re-entry poster (Instagram re-serves carousels mid-deck) and engineer one screenshot-and-send-worthy line per deck (a salary number, a brutal one-liner, a single tool name) to farm sends.

10. Boost only decks that already over-indexed on SAVES and SENDS in their first 48 hours; start 5 to 20 USD via the Boost button; never boost cold.

11. Add a `just linkedin-pdf` recipe: shotpls PNG -> JPEG q92 -> `img2pdf --imgsize 144dpi -o deck.pdf slide-*.jpg`. Keep the cover's hook/logo/CTA out of the bottom 15 to 20 percent (LinkedIn banner) and off the right edge; add a "3 of 8" progress cue; put the CTA on-slide or in caption, never as an external link.

12. Instrument the funnel by deck: tag every commenter with deck+keyword+outcome (clicked/gave email/converted) so decks rank by funnel yield, not vanity reach. Hold to 85 to 92 percent DM open, 40 to 70 percent CTR, 60 to 85 percent email capture; if far under, the DM copy is the problem not the deck.

## OPEN QUESTIONS

- No verbatim Mosseri quote with an exact date+URL was captured by any scout or head verification (X/Threads refuse fetch); the strongest quotes come via financial-marketer.com paraphrasing. Acceptable for internal strategy, do not publish the quote as primary-sourced.
- The primary Mosseri source for "sends 3 to 5x likes" was not found. Direction is solid; the multiplier stays folklore until a primary surfaces.
- No cybersecurity/IT-cert-niche-specific benchmark exists for saves-per-slide, sends-per-slide, or comment-to-DM conversion. All public data is broad "education/schools" or business-coach case studies. Carter's own Insights are the only niche-resolution truth, which is exactly why directive 12 (instrumentation) matters.
- The exact 5-hashtag enforcement mechanism (hard block vs silent removal vs suppression) is not pinned to an Instagram primary. Treat "over 5 = suppressed" as the safe operating rule.
- Whether LinkedIn server-side re-compresses uploaded PDF pages is unconfirmed, so do not over-invest above JPEG q92. A one-time visual QA on a real posted deck settles both this and the exact cover safe-zone geometry.
- img2pdf's interaction with any DPI chunk shotpls may stamp into the PNG/JPEG is untested. Two-minute check once in the rig: render one deck, confirm the PDF mediabox reads 1080x1350 pt.
- No confirmation whether Instagram has shipped or is testing native comment-to-DM automation or a carousel trial. Absence of evidence, not proof. Re-check quarterly.
