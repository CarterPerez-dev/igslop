# DIVISION G: Adjacent-Platform Carousel Intelligence (Leading Indicators for Instagram)

Compiled 2026-07-10 (Wave 2, supersedes the Wave 1 draft). Sources: four scout sweeps (g1 TikTok photo mode, g2 LinkedIn PDF carousels, g3 Pinterest/X/Threads, g4 cross-platform systems) plus division-head verification searches. Recency prioritized April to July 2026. Everything older is labeled background.

Note on the Wave 1 correction: the prior draft stated "X has no swipeable carousel (auto 2x2 grid only)." That is now wrong. Scout g3 confirms X shipped native organic swipeable carousels across April to July 2026 (SocialBee dated log). This report reflects the corrected finding.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Four adjacent platforms independently confirm the poster pivot and none contradict it. The winning unit everywhere is a composed real-artifact poster with near-zero body copy, and the currency everywhere is the SAVE. Pinterest ran a controlled A/B (collage 87% of saves vs text-overlay 6% vs single-image 7%), which mirrors Carter's own 10x saves gap. TikTok's neutral large-sample data (Metricool, 2.3M posts) says carousels are the WEAK format at the aggregate but flags EDUCATION as the one exception pulling 100x to 200x, and TikTok's design meta (5 to 10 slides, dark, huge type, one idea per slide) is real-world proof the busy newspaper slide loses. LinkedIn reach stabilized in 2026 (down just 2% QoQ), documents are still the top format, and the top 1% shifted to single-frame infographics, validating the poster thesis on a second platform. His 1080x1350 canvas is already native for both Instagram AND LinkedIn, so one build ships two outputs with zero re-layout; TikTok 9:16 and the profile-grid 1:1 crop are the only real gaps, both closable with small shotpls additions. Two cross-post channels that did not exist a year ago are now live: X native carousels (April to July 2026) and Threads carousels, both eating the exact 4:5 poster slides he renders. The hard constraint: LinkedIn PDFs are strictly static, so animation is an Instagram-only enhancement layer, never load-bearing.

## THE FINDINGS THAT MATTER (ranked)

### 1. The collage-pin A/B is our strongest external proof, and it defines the winning UNIT (g3)
Jared Bauman's controlled Pinterest experiment (Better Marketing / Medium): collage pins (multiple real images composed into one graphic) took 73% of impressions, 87% of saves, 81% of outbound clicks. Text-overlay pins got 6% of saves. Pure single-image got 7%. Verbatim verdict: "Collage Pins are my most effective Pin Design."
Source: https://medium.com/better-marketing/which-pin-design-type-works-best-on-pinterest-my-results-4d8eb9b540bb
This is TWO datasets, same verdict: Bauman's ~10x saves gap for composed real imagery matches Carter's own 90-day gap (image-artifact decks 2,700 to 6,600 saves vs text-only 100 to 570). The winning unit is not one photo and not a text template. It is 2 to 4 REAL artifacts arranged as a composition, capped at 4 so it never re-becomes a newspaper (Social Champ collage cap, https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/pinterest-pin-size/).

### 2. Saves are the shared currency across every adjacent platform (g2, g3)
Pinterest: saves are the #1 ranking signal, clicks #2 (GTR, Sprout, RankMyPin). LinkedIn: one save drives ~5x the reach of a like and ~2x a comment (van der Blom); carousels have the highest save rate of any format at 29.2% (Saywhat Q1 2026 via Kara Redman). Threads: image posts cited at +41% save rate vs text (Replia, single-source). Same mechanic as Carter's Instagram save wins. DIRECTIVE-LEVEL: design each slide to be SAVED as a reference object, not READ in-feed.
Sources: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/pinterest-algorithm/ , g2 file (van der Blom + Saywhat), https://replia.net/blog/threads-image-post-guide

### 3. TikTok's central contradiction, and the exception that is our niche (g1)
Two camps disagree, flag it explicitly. Carousel-tool vendors (ReelBase, TokPortal) claim photo mode gets 5x to 6.2x more reach than video. The neutral large-sample studies say the OPPOSITE at the aggregate: Metricool (2,314,756 posts, 92,000+ accounts, released 2026-05-12) found VIDEO gets ~5.6x more views and ~7.8x more interactions than image/carousel on TikTok, and per-post carousel views FELL 23% while interactions fell 62% YoY. Buffer (52M+ posts): TikTok video 3.39% vs carousel 1.92% median engagement.
THE EXCEPTION: Metricool's strategy page states "a handful of beauty, fitness, and EDUCATION accounts are pulling 100x to 200x the average engagement of their size category with carousels," education flagged at 5.78% engagement. CertGames is on the right side of the exception. CAVEAT: no account names or methodology, so it is a directional outlier headline, not rigorous.
Resolution for us: photo mode is NOT a universal cheat code (vendor hype); it IS disproportionately strong for reference-value educational content, which is what we ship. TikTok is a distribution multiplier and early-signal lab, not the primary carousel home.

### 4. Carousels are worth MORE on Instagram than anywhere else (g1)
Buffer: IG carousel 6.9% median engagement is the top IG format (vs single image 4.4%, Reels 3.3%). Metricool 2026 IG study (24.3M posts, 375K accounts, ~May 2026): Instagram earns ~4.7x more views and ~5.8x more interactions on carousels than TikTok, and IG carousels get 9x more saves than single images. Instagram stays the primary carousel home. Everything else is repurposing.

### 5. LinkedIn reach STABILIZED in 2026 and the top 1% moved to single-frame infographics (g2)
Saywhat State of the Algorithm Q1 2026 (397,605 posts, Will McTighe): median impressions down 68% from the Q3 2023 peak but down just 2% Q4-2025 to Q1-2026. The "reach crashed" story is 2023-2024, not now. Documents remain the strongest FORMAT (~6.6% engagement, Socialinsider 1M posts). THE 2026 WRINKLE: infographics overtook multi-slide carousels among top 1% posts at roughly 28% share (28.6% Dataslayer, 27.4% Kara Redman, same report). Both still beat text ~3x. A single strong poster frame is now the strongest visual play on LinkedIn too.
Named-source confirmation of the poster direction: Forbes (Jodie Cook, 2026-04-22, "5 LinkedIn Carousel Structures That Consistently Go Viral") lists bold-claim opening, before/after with specific metrics, one-mistake-per-slide, step-by-step with jargon removed, and unpopular-opinion, with the rule "keep text minimal" and "clean and readable." A named LinkedIn tech creator cited for exactly this style: Ruben Hassid (bold typography, high contrast, minimal text, one idea per slide, via https://carouselli.com/blog/best-linkedin-carousel-examples ).
Sources: g2 file (Saywhat, Socialinsider, Dataslayer), https://www.forbes.com/sites/jodiecook/2026/04/22/5-linkedin-carousel-structures-that-consistently-go-viral/

### 6. His canvas is already native for the two platforms that matter most (g2, g4)
1080x1350 (4:5) is the recommended size for BOTH Instagram AND LinkedIn PDF documents. Zero re-layout. One build, two outputs. The cross-platform problem reduces to just (a) TikTok 9:16 and (b) Instagram's profile-grid 1:1 crop.
Sources: supaslides.app/blog/carousel-dimensions-guide, oktopost + trymypost LinkedIn corpus (g2, g4)

### 7. Two new swipeable homes exist that did not a year ago: X and Threads native carousels (g3)
X shipped native organic Carousels in 2026 (SocialBee dated log): April iOS displays 2+ images as a swipeable carousel, June wider rollout, July desktop shows 4+ images as carousels. 2 to 6 assets, cannot mix photo+video. Threads has a carousel up to 10 images, sweet spot 2 to 4 (completion drops sharply after slide 4), best ratio 4:5 1080x1350 (identical to IG). Both eat the exact poster slides he already renders.
Sources: https://socialbee.com/blog/twitter-updates/ , https://www.socialnewsdesk.com/blog/how-to-use-twitters-new-carousels-feature-for-organic-tweets/ , https://replia.net/blog/threads-image-post-guide

### 8. The motion-slide bet is aligned with the platform roadmap (g1, verified)
CONFIRMED SHIPPED: Instagram native mixed-media carousels (images + video in one carousel) are live and outperform image-only, 2.33% engagement vs 1.80% (verified 2026-07-10 via https://embedsocial.com/blog/new-instagram-features-2026/ and contentdrips 2026 guide). TikTok's Live Photo trick lets a short looping motion slide sit inside a static Photo Mode carousel. Both platforms converge on swipeable decks that mix poster slides with 1 to 2 motion slides. Instagram is also redistributing carousels-with-music in the Reels tab (confirmed), though carousels-fully-INSIDE-Reels as a single post remains fuzzier.
CONTRADICTION / LOW CONFIDENCE: one blog (TryMyPost, 2026-03-06) claims subtle first-slide motion lifts stop-scroll "up to 22%," but it traces to no primary study and no other source corroborates it. Treat as unverified directional, do NOT quote as fact. There is NO measured completion-rate lift for a motion slide vs an all-static deck anywhere found.

### 9. Design specs converge identically across all four platforms (g1, g2, g3, g4)
Dark background (#121212, not pure black, ~60% of the palette), one accent, one focal point, huge high-contrast type, 1 to 5 lines per slide, one idea per slide. Text floors: cover headline 90 to 170px on a 1080 canvas, body 48 to 64px, 28px hard bottom for any real text (LinkedIn's stated floor), anything smaller is fine print. Word budget: cover under ~40 characters / <=10 words, interior slides one discrete point. Canva 2026 "Imperfect by Design": searches for "brutalist design," "bold typography," "type poster" rose 77% YoY, externally validating the huge-hero-type direction. Two named 2026 trend languages are purpose-built for cyber: Surveillance Design (CCTV grids, HUDs, crosshairs, mono-utility type) and Blueprint Design (exploded labeled diagram of one object), both anti-newspaper by construction (Kittl, https://www.kittl.com/blogs/graphic-design-trends-2026/ ).

### 10. Slide count is the one real per-platform divergence (g1, g2)
TikTok: 5 to 10, tighter, because it hits cold FYP strangers. Instagram: 5 to 8 tight punchy posters. LinkedIn: 8 to 12, because extra swipes buy dwell time (its top ranking signal) and users read deeper. Same visual system, three assemblies differing on COUNT and CAPTION, not design. LinkedIn caption is longer and analytical with an open-question CTA and 0 to 1 hashtag (3+ hashtags cost 71% reach per Saywhat); Instagram CTA is save/comment-keyword; Threads pairs the poster with a real question caption (image + question = 3.1x replies) and you reply in the first 30 to 90 minutes.

### 11. The open lane: nobody re-flows content to each platform's safe zone (g4)
Every tool (including the closest analog, github.com/itchernetski/threads-carousel-claude-skill) only RESIZES the canvas per platform. None RE-FLOW content into each platform's distinct safe zone (uniform 80px padding everywhere, no notch or right-rail compensation). A left-aligned 4:5 deck padded to TikTok 9:16 bleeds into the ~164px right icon rail and ~324px bottom bar. His HTML-per-slide pipeline CAN re-flow via CSS-variable swaps because each slide is real logic, not a flattened template. This is a defensible differentiator worth a few dozen lines, not a rewrite. Separately, the entire top tier of cyber X accounts (Krebs, Troy Hunt, Schneier, Mikko, Dan Goodin) is text/journalist-first with essentially no designed graphics, so poster-grade cyber education graphics own an empty lane on X.

## DIRECTIVES FOR THE REBUILD (numbered, concrete)

1. Make the deck DEFAULT the composed-real-artifact poster: 2 to 4 real artifacts (badge renders, book covers, tool screenshots, exploded labeled diagrams) arranged as a designed composition, hard-capped at 4 elements. This is the single change with the strongest cross-platform proof (Pinterest 87% of saves + Carter's own 10x gap). Deprecate the text-only template deck.

2. Design every slide to be SAVED, not read. One artifact, huge legible labels, one focal point, one idea. Saves are the ranking currency on Pinterest, LinkedIn, Threads and Instagram simultaneously. Target the 0.5%+ save-to-impression floor.

3. Bake the shared design spec into make-carousel as guardrails: dark #121212 base (~60% of palette) + one accent, cover headline 90 to 170px, body 48 to 64px, 28px hard floor, cover under ~40 characters / <=10 words, interior slides one discrete point, high contrast mandatory. High-contrast beat complex backgrounds by 40%; minimalism raised completion 12%.

4. Enforce the INTERSECTION SAFE BOX: keep every load-bearing element inside the central ~1000x1000 square and above the bottom third. That single rule survives IG feed 4:5, IG profile-grid 1:1 crop (cuts top/bottom 270px each), LinkedIn 4:5, and a center-padded TikTok 9:16. Add a dev-only CSS guide toggle (grid-safe 1080x1080 + UI-safe margins).

5. Ship LinkedIn nearly for free: stitch the same shotpls PNGs into a multi-image PDF (img2pdf or Pillow, keep under 10MB, export PDF-Standard not PDF-Print). Author dual-destined decks to the stricter 80px margin so IG (40px) and LinkedIn (80px) are both satisfied.

6. Add a two-tier TikTok 9:16 export: cheap tier pads interior slides onto 1080x1920 via Sharp/Pillow using the slide's own surface color (not letterbox); careful tier gives the COVER a real data-ratio="9x16" mode that clears the right ~164px icon rail and bottom ~324px bar. Add a font-weight >=500 lint when exporting to TikTok (compression blurs hairline weights, line-height ~1.3).

7. Make every slide read perfectly as a STILL first. Animation/motion is an Instagram-only enhancement layer over a static base, never load-bearing, because LinkedIn PDFs kill all motion and X carousels cannot mix photo+video. Build the static poster, render it for IG-static + LinkedIn-PDF, then optionally animate a copy for IG only.

8. Prioritize exactly ONE hero motion slide per deck (the cover or the payoff) using Instagram's confirmed-shipped mixed-media carousel (2.33% vs 1.80% image-only). Keep the rest static posters. Do not gimmick every slide; there is no measured lift for that and the platform data rewards restraint.

9. Ship one deck to three swipeable homes with a per-surface flag: uniform aspect ratio across all slides (mandatory on X or it crops), cap heavy slides at 2 to 4 for Threads completion, optionally render a square 1080x1080 cut of the four strongest slides for the legacy X 2x2 grid (all four same ratio, content centered).

10. Build the two named cyber-native trend languages as reusable slide templates: Surveillance Design (HUD/crosshair/grid/mono-utility frame) and Blueprint Design (one object exploded and labeled with callouts). Both enforce a single focal point by construction and own an empty visual lane in the cyber niche.

11. Assemble three outputs from one build, differing only on COUNT and CAPTION: Instagram 5 to 8 slides + save/comment-keyword CTA; TikTok 5 to 10 slides + audio + FYP-swipe read; LinkedIn 8 to 12 slides + long analytical caption + open-question CTA + 0 to 1 hashtag. Same visual system, three assemblies.

12. Use TikTok as the fast test lab. Trend/format ideas migrate TikTok-to-IG in ~1 to 2 weeks, not 6 to 12 months. Ship a deck to TikTok first for cheap cold-FYP completion-rate signal, then push winners to Instagram within days while the topic is warm.

## OPEN QUESTIONS

1. No verified real-name cyber/tech creator with HARD adjacent-platform poster-carousel numbers. TikTok/X post pages refuse WebFetch; search surfaced only text-first journalists (confirming the open lane) and vendor-study archetype handles (@sarahsells, @gadgetguy) that are likely illustrative, not real. The empty lane is itself the finding, but we have no exemplar to copy.

2. No MEASURED completion-rate or save lift for a motion/Live-Photo slide vs an all-static deck. The technique is documented on both platforms; its performance delta is unquantified anywhere. The one "22% stop-scroll" claim (TryMyPost, 2026-03-06) is single-source with no primary study and should not be treated as fact. Worth an internal A/B once the pipeline can render one.

3. The Metricool 100x-to-200x education-niche claim has no account names, slide specs, or methodology. Directionally supportive, not rigorous.

4. Absolute engagement-rate numbers disagree massively across firms (LinkedIn documents cited as both 6.6% and 1.92%) because each computes the denominator differently. Only the relative ratios and format ordering are trustworthy. The infographic top-1% share is ~28% (28.6% vs 27.4% from the same Saywhat report); the raw Saywhat PDF could not be opened to pin the exact figure.

5. No official Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn primary-doc safe-zone spec was fetched; every pixel figure is strongly cross-corroborated marketing-tool-blog consensus, not primary. TikTok right-rail width varies by source (~120px Zeely vs ~164px HouseOfMarketers); we use the wider 164px as the safe assumption.

6. Carousels-fully-INSIDE-Reels as a single post is confirmed only as far as "carousels-with-music redistributed in the Reels tab." The native mixed-media carousel (images+video in a standard carousel) IS confirmed shipped; the deeper Reels-carousel hybrid remains fuzzy.

7. No hard measured penalty for cross-posting an identical deck vs a natively-reframed one in 2026. The reframe-per-platform advice is qualitative consensus, not a quantified reach hit.
